Every sunrise needs a helping hand.

SAHARA is the social welfare society of IIT Madras. We bring students together to serve the communities around us with time, skills and care.

See our eventsJoin SAHARA

What SAHARA stands for

SAHARA means support. We turn the energy of a campus into steady help for people who need it, and we build students who care along the way.

Mission

Run welfare drives, education programmes and awareness campaigns that make a real difference to local communities.

Vision

A campus where serving others is part of student life, and where every project leaves a lasting benefit.

Values

Compassion, consistency and teamwork. We show up, listen first and finish what we start.
