SAHARA is the social welfare society of IIT Madras. We bring students together to serve the communities around us with time, skills and care.

What SAHARA stands for

We are a student-driven initiative creating meaningful impact in education, healthcare, environment, public policy, mental health, rural development and digital inclusion. Our aim is to nurture socially conscious leaders who combine compassion with action, and ideas with implementation.

Mission

Empower students to tackle real-world challenges through social innovation, consulting and technology-led projects.

Vision

A generation of changemakers who grow as leaders, thinkers and collaborators, and who make a tangible difference.

Values

Compassion, consistency and teamwork. We show up, listen first and finish what we start.
